# Code of Conduct

Boardly should be a focused, respectful space for building software and discussing project work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, respectful, and constructive.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Give useful context when reporting problems or proposing changes.
- Assume public comments may be read by future contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Spam, trolling, or intentionally disruptive comments.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Publishing exploit details for unresolved security issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments and may restrict access when behavior harms the project or contributors.
